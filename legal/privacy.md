# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 12 November 2026

Plan Your Shot ("the Service") is operated by Swaraj Panda ("we", "us"). This policy explains what we collect, why, and what you can do about it. If you have questions, contact us at swarajxpanda@gmail.com.

## What we collect

**Account information.** When you sign in with Google we receive your name, email address, profile picture and a Google account identifier. We do not receive your Google password and we do not request access to your Gmail, Drive or any other Google data.

**Your content.** The screenplay files you upload, the text we extract from them, every saved version of a screenplay, your conversations with the assistant, suggested edits, and your shot lists.

**Usage records.** A log of the AI requests you make (type of request, time, size, and whether it succeeded), which we use to enforce usage limits and keep the Service running.

**Technical data.** One cookie that keeps you signed in (`pys_session`, valid for up to 30 days), and standard server logs such as IP address and browser type. If something breaks, an error report may be sent to our error-monitoring provider. We do not use advertising or third-party analytics cookies.

## How we use it

- To provide the Service: store your screenplays, answer your questions about them, prepare edits you ask for, and generate and save shot lists.
- To secure the Service, prevent abuse and enforce usage limits.
- To diagnose and fix problems.
- To contact you about your account or important changes to the Service.

We do not sell your information. We do not use your screenplays, conversations or shot lists to train AI models, and we do not read them except when needed to fix a problem you have reported, to investigate suspected abuse, or when required by law.

## AI processing

To answer questions, prepare edits and generate shot lists, the Service sends the text of your screenplay, your messages and your current shot list to a third-party AI model provider, currently Google (Gemini API, paid tier).

Under that provider's terms for its paid API, the content we send is not used to improve the provider's products. The provider may log requests and responses for a limited period to detect misuse, and may process data in any country where it operates.

## Who we share it with

Only the service providers we need to run the Service, each of which processes data on our behalf:

- **AI model provider:** Google (Gemini API)
- **Hosting and database:** our cloud hosting and database providers
- **Sign-in:** Google
- **Error monitoring:** our error-monitoring provider

We may also disclose information if required by law, or to protect the rights, safety or property of our users or ourselves. If the Service is ever transferred to another operator, we will tell you before your information moves.

## Where it is stored

Your data is stored on servers operated by our hosting provider and may be processed in other countries by the providers listed above.

## How long we keep it

We keep your account and content until you delete them. You can delete an individual screenplay at any time, which removes its file, versions, conversations and shot list. You can delete your whole account from the account menu, which removes everything associated with it. Deleted data is removed from our live systems immediately and from backups within 30 days. Server logs are kept for up to 30 days.

## Your choices and rights

You can view, download and delete your content inside the Service. You can ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, or to delete it by writing to swarajxpanda@gmail.com; we will respond within 30 days. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local law, including the right to complain to your data protection authority.

## Security

Connections to the Service are encrypted (HTTPS). Each account can only access its own screenplays, conversations and shot lists. Uploaded files are stored privately and are never publicly accessible. No system is perfectly secure; if we learn of a breach affecting your data we will notify you.

## Age

The Service is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

## Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will notify you in the Service or by email before the change takes effect. The date at the top shows when it was last updated.
